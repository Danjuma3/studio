# **App Name**: EkoPlateCost

## Core Features:

- Ingredient Inventory: Enter and manage ingredients, including names, 'Bulk' and 'Retail' unit prices, and defined units of measure. Stores data for persistent access.
- Recipe Composer: Create and edit recipes by selecting ingredients from the inventory and specifying their quantities.
- Dynamic Recipe Cost Calculator: View real-time total cost for any recipe, with an interactive toggle to switch between 'Bulk' and 'Retail' ingredient pricing to compare costs.
- Cost Optimization Insight Tool: An AI-powered tool that analyzes ingredient usage patterns and price data to suggest optimal procurement strategies (e.g., when to buy in bulk) to maximize cost savings for the restaurant.
- Weekly Market Price Update: Allows users to automatically fetch or manually input weekly market prices for key ingredients to ensure cost calculations reflect current market realities.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A vibrant, fresh green (#2BB34D) that signifies growth and nature, reflecting the freshness of ingredients and the efficient operation of a restaurant. This will be used for key interactive elements and branding.
- Background color: A subtle, light green-white (#F2F8F4) provides a clean and expansive canvas, ensuring readability and evoking a sense of hygiene and clarity suitable for food cost management.
- Accent color: A lively yellow-green (#89EE4D) chosen for calls-to-action and important highlights, designed to draw attention without being overwhelming, creating an energetic and intuitive user experience.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter' (sans-serif), chosen for its modern, clean, and highly legible characteristics, ensuring all numerical data and recipe details are clear and easy to read for restaurant staff.
- Utilize a suite of simple, modern, line-based icons relevant to food, measurements, and cost management, maintaining a cohesive and intuitive visual language across the application.
- Employ a clear and structured grid-based layout with generous white space to present complex data in an easily digestible format, emphasizing clear forms for ingredient and recipe entry, and prominent display for cost calculations.
- Incorporate subtle micro-interactions and smooth transitions, especially during the 'Bulk vs. Retail' pricing toggle and data input, to provide responsive feedback and enhance user engagement.
